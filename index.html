Your robot does not need to work everywhere.
It needs to work at your place.

Instead of learning a general solution, we provide a general learning method for user-specific solutions. Taught its own home and the tasks that matter to you, by the people who live there.

Generalist approach

  • designed for broad environments
  • large-scale pretraining
  • keeps going when things change
  • predefined task sequences
  • generic task execution

AhaRo approach

  • adapted to one specific home
  • a few demonstrations from you
  • asks when confidence is low
  • composes learned skills for new requests
  • your preferences, remembered

Show it once.

All in plain language: no app, nothing to program. You talk to AhaRo as you would to a helper, and it talks back. Teaching is not setup. It is everyday use, and it gets shorter each time.

YouThis is my home. Here is the fridge, and this is how you open it.

Skills it has learned

  • cups, bottles, cloths, whatever ends up on the floor
  • stable placement on a shelf, a table or in the sink, without tipping
  • the cloth, the fruit bowl, your glasses, wherever they were last
  • the fridge needs a firm pull, the bedroom door swings, the balcony door slides
  • on its own, when the battery runs low

New requests, no new teaching

  • find the cloth, bring it, take the cup to the sink
  • open the fridge, pick up the juice, open the balcony door, bring it out
  • pick up the cups, place them by the sink, dock
  • find it where it saw it last, pick it up, bring it
  • checks what it saw this morning and answers. And at 13:00, unasked: opens the drawer, picks up the pills, brings them to you

Built for a home, not a factory.

Light enough to lift into a car, slim enough for any doorway. Sized for a flat, not a production line.

  • 1

    A depth camera sees the room, the objects in it, and your gestures. When you point, it works out what you mean.

  • 2

    Lightweight arms with two-finger grippers, sized for cups, bottles, handles and switches. They give way if they meet something unexpected.

  • 3

    Both arms ride the column, so AhaRo reaches a remote on the floor and a cup on the counter.

  • 4

    A little bigger than a robot vacuum. Carries the computer and battery on two drive wheels and casters, low to the ground.

What it does at home.

Everyday abilities to start with, more as you teach it. You ask in plain language; it answers the same way.

  • Brings what you name or point at. Bring me my glasses.

  • Everyday speech. Asks back when unclear, tells you what it is about to do.

  • Learns your layout and moves between rooms on its own.

  • Picks up, places, tidies, puts away.

  • It goes and looks, then tells you. Where did I leave the keys?

  • Medication, meals, appointments. Brings what the reminder is about.

  • Swinging or sliding, once it knows which is which.

  • Which pot, how much, how often, once you have shown it.

  • Fills the bowl on schedule, throws the ball when you are out.

  • Ask from anywhere, it goes to look. Is the stove off?

  • Docks on its own when the battery runs low.

  • Someone you choose sees what was done, adjusts the schedule, and steps in from their phone when asked.

From passive to active intellect.

AhaRo knows what it has learned, how confident it is, and when it needs help. Today's robot AI recognises and imitates well, but it does not know when it is unsure and does not plan past the next step. AhaRo adds that proactive part, built on trustworthy AI methods.

For technical readers

Where most robot AI stops

  • it has read a lot
  • it can tell a cup from a bottle
  • it can copy a movement
  • it has a rough idea how things move

Where AhaRo starts

  • it knows when it is unsure
  • it knows when to hand over to you
  • it learns from you as you go
  • it thinks a task through before acting

A hybrid cognitive architecture, neuro-symbolic in research terms: learned skills for the physical world, a step-by-step planner that chains them into longer sequences, and a memory of your home. The memory holds where things are, how they work and how you like them handled.

How it stays safe

Every skill knows how confident it is. Below its confidence threshold AhaRo stops and asks you, or someone you trust, from wherever they are. It does not finish a task it is unsure about on its own.
